# 🧟‍♂️ ZombieFarm

ZombieFarm is a mobile and desktop idle shooter built in Unity 6, featuring intense wave action, epic boss battles, survivor upgrades, a global leaderboard, player login, and persistent online saves.

---

## 🎮 Features

- Idle & Active Play: Tap to shoot, deploy auto-firing survivors, and progress even while away.
- Boss Fights: Special boss waves. Tap fast for bonuses!
- Global Leaderboard: Compete in real time via Firebase.
- Secure Registration & Login: Passwords are SHA-256 hashed before upload.
- Shop & Power Ups: Recruit, upgrade, and sell survivors strategically.
- Saves & Stats: All progress and stats are saved online.
- Modern UI: Built with TextMeshPro and Unity UI.
- Multi-Platform: Android APK & desktop builds.
- Tested on Unity 6 (6000.0.25f1)

## 🕹️ How to Play

- Register or login for persistent online progress.
- Tap zombies or let survivors shoot automatically.
- Earn, upgrade, and manage resources in the shop.
- Defeat boss zombies for major rewards.
- Compete on the global leaderboard!
